Kamu Tales Pro

Subscribe once and use the full publishable story catalog on web, iOS, and Android with the same TAuth identity.

Effective
August 13, 2026
Last updated

Plan and Price

Kamu Tales Pro is $4.99 USD per month through web checkout, before any applicable tax. Apple App Store and Google Play display the localized price that applies before a native purchase.

The subscription renews monthly until canceled. There is no free trial in the current offer. The 40-story original-language free catalog remains available without a subscription.

What Is Included

An active subscription unlocks the full publishable Kamu story catalog in addition to the 40 free stories. Subscriber access includes the available text, artwork, audio, read-along, and video experiences attached to those stories.

New publishable stories and accepted media may be added over time. Catalog availability can vary when rights, quality, safety, or technical requirements change.

One Subscription Across Platforms

Kamu binds subscription access to the TAuth identity used when a purchase is verified. After subscribing on web, iOS, or Android, sign in with that same TAuth identity on the other platforms to use the entitlement there.

Use restore or refresh after signing in when access has not appeared yet. A different TAuth identity is a different Kamu account, even when the device uses the same Apple ID, Google account, or payment email.

Purchase and Management

Sign in from the account control, then choose Subscribe. Web checkout is billed by Paddle; iOS checkout is billed by Apple; Android checkout is billed by Google Play. The billing platform confirms the exact price, tax, and renewal terms before payment.

Manage or cancel the subscription through the platform that bills it. Cancellation stops the next renewal and normally preserves access until the paid period ends. Before buying on another platform, sign in with the original TAuth identity and restore or refresh.

Support and Policies

For product, identity, or entitlement-delivery help, email support@mprlab.com. The Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Refund and Cancellation Policy are linked from every Kamu page.
