Plan and Price

The web plan is $4.99 USD per month before applicable tax. Billing starts at purchase and renews monthly until canceled.

The free catalog includes 40 original-language stories and is open without an account.

Catalog Availability

Stories and media may change as new titles become available or as publication rights, safety, or technical requirements change.

One Subscription Across Platforms

Access on each mobile platform starts when Kamu is released in that store. Sign in with the same Kamu account to use an active subscription across supported platforms.

The App Store and Google Play show the local price and billing terms before a native purchase.

Purchase and Management

When web checkout is available, sign in to Kamu and choose Subscribe in Settings. Paddle handles web billing, cancellation, and refund requests.

Manage or cancel your subscription through the billing provider. Cancellation stops the next renewal and normally preserves access until the paid period ends.

If access is missing, sign in with the Kamu account used for the purchase. For a native purchase, use the store restore option or email support@mprlab.com for help.

Support and Policies

For product, identity, or entitlement-delivery help, email support@mprlab.com. The Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Refund and Cancellation Policy are linked from every Kamu page.
