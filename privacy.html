Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Fairy Tales by Kamu handles information when families browse the free catalog, sign in, subscribe, use subscriber content, or contact us.

Effective
August 13, 2026
Last updated
2026-10-02

Overview

Fairy Tales by Kamu provides cleaned and adapted fairy tales, illustrations, audio readings, read-along timing, and story video links or embeds on the web, iOS, and Android.

The free catalog does not require an account. A TAuth account is required to buy, restore, or use Kamu Tales Pro across platforms. The account belongs to the parent or guardian. Children do not need an account for public stories.

Information We Process

Website and media hosts receive network requests, including an IP address, request path, browser information, and request time. Hosting and security logs can contain this information. This version does not load Google Analytics or LoopAware.

When you sign in, TAuth provides a stable account identifier and the basic profile information authorized by the sign-in provider. Kamu uses that identifier to keep your account and subscription separate from every other user.

For subscription access, Kamu and RevenueCat process the stable customer identifier tauth:<user-id>, the purchase platform, product and entitlement identifiers, transaction references, purchase and expiration times, renewal state, and subscription-management URL. This lets one verified entitlement follow the same TAuth identity across web, iOS, and Android.

If you send feedback or contact us, we process the message contents and any contact details you choose to include.

When you use embedded or linked media, third-party services such as YouTube may receive interaction, device, and network information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Device Permissions and Sensitive Categories

Kamu does not ask for phone contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, SMS, call logs, health data, store-account passwords, or full payment-card details. Apple, Google, or Paddle processes payment credentials for purchases made through its platform. If you include sensitive information in a feedback message or email, we process it only as part of that message.

Website reading controls use page memory. A page reload starts in Read mode. Browser Back and Forward can restore the earlier page state. The reader does not save these selections or load earlier saved selections. Clear local preferences removes earlier Kamu reading and language records from this browser. Parent account services and external media can use their own storage.

How We Use Information

We use necessary technical information for content delivery and service security. We use parent account information for login and subscription access. We use support messages to respond to the sender.

We do not sell personal information and do not use the site data we control to build third-party advertising profiles.

Third-Party Services

Kamu uses TAuth for parent identity and RevenueCat for subscription status. Apple App Store, Google Play, and Paddle process purchases. Website and API hosts deliver content. Google Identity and UI assets from jsDelivr load after you open the account area.

Apple, Google, Paddle, and RevenueCat process purchase and subscription information under their own privacy terms. Paddle is the merchant of record for web purchases. Kamu does not receive your full payment-card details from those providers.

The site may also link to podcast platforms such as Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and Amazon Music. Those links take visitors to external services governed by their own policies.

Google and YouTube services are governed by their own policies, including Google's privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy.

Those providers may process technical data according to their own policies. We choose these services to operate the public site, not to require visitor accounts on Fairy Tales by Kamu.

Browser Do Not Track and Third-Party Collection

Kamu's website code does not change its data processing in response to browser Do Not Track signals. Google Analytics and LoopAware remain disabled for every visitor, with or without that signal. Third-party services control their own response to browser signals.

Parent actions can load Google Identity or open external media. Google's privacy policy describes possible collection of personal information about activity over time and across different websites, including through YouTube. Collection depends on the service and account settings. See Google's privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy.

Google OAuth and Google API Data

The public story catalog does not require Google sign-in. A parent can choose Google sign-in within the parent area. Use your own adult account and review the provider permissions.

Consumer Google sign-in requests openid, profile, and email. These permissions identify the parent account. The child experience does not request Google or YouTube channel access. Operator publication credentials are separate from consumer login.

We use Google user data only to provide and improve prominent user-facing connected-account features, troubleshoot and secure those features, prevent abuse, and comply with law. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness.

We store Google user data only as long as needed for the authorized feature, security, legal, abuse-prevention, and recordkeeping purposes. Any stored Google authorization tokens are treated as sensitive credentials.

We do not transfer Google user data except to service providers needed to operate the requested feature, for security or legal reasons, as part of a business transaction with required consent where applicable, or at the user's explicit direction. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google's API user-data policy, including the Limited Use requirements: Google API Services User Data Policy.

You can revoke access from the Google connected apps and services page: Google connected apps and services page. You may also request deletion of stored Google-authorized data by emailing support@mprlab.com.

Children and Families

The product is for parents who select stories for children. Parent accounts and local interface preferences support this model. Kamu does not create child accounts or synchronize child activity. Parents must use their own account for login and purchases.

Public stories remain available without login. Account controls, sharing, and external links start when you select them. Use your own parent account. Do not submit a child name, email, birth date, image, or voice. Parents can contact support about information a child submits.

Retention and Requests

We keep account, entitlement, operational, and support information only as long as necessary for the stated purposes or required retention. Payment platforms may retain their own transaction records under their policies and legal obligations.

The parent area gives your current profile and subscription status after login. Download this information on the website or share it from the mobile app. This information excludes local interface preferences and reading activity. Request other records separately.

A separate parent control requests RevenueCat customer and billing records. It requires a qualified provider connection. An unavailable or incomplete response gives an error. These records exclude other processors, internal logs, and backups. Downloading records does not delete an account.

To request access, correction, or deletion, email support@mprlab.com. For legal requests, email legal@mprlab.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site, providers, or legal requirements change. The updated page will show the current effective and last-updated dates.
