Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Fairy Tales by Kamu handles information when families read, listen to, watch, or send feedback through the public website.

Effective
June 5, 2026
Last updated
2026-06-05

Overview

Fairy Tales by Kamu is a public website for cleaned and adapted fairy tales, illustrations, audio readings, read-along timing, and story video links or embeds.

The site does not require visitor accounts. We process only the information needed to operate, secure, measure, and improve the website and to respond when someone contacts us.

Information We Process

We may process technical website data such as pages visited, referrers, browser and device details, approximate timestamps, IP-derived network information, performance data, and error diagnostics.

If you send feedback or contact us, we process the message contents and any contact details you choose to include.

When you use embedded or linked media, third-party services such as YouTube may receive interaction, device, and network information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Device Permissions and Sensitive Categories

The public website does not ask for phone number, contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, SMS, call logs, health data, or payment-card data. If you include any of that information in a feedback message or email, we process it only as part of that message.

The website uses ordinary browser storage only for visitor-controlled reading preferences and playback experience. It does not use device permissions to collect data from a phone, tablet, or computer.

How We Use Information

We use website and feedback information to keep the site working, understand which stories and pages are useful, troubleshoot defects, prevent abuse, respond to messages, and improve the reading, listening, and watching experience.

We do not sell personal information and do not use the site data we control to build third-party advertising profiles.

Third-Party Services

The site may use hosting, content delivery, analytics, feedback, and media services, including LoopAware for feedback and traffic measurement and YouTube or YouTube-nocookie embeds for story videos.

The site may also link to podcast platforms such as Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and Amazon Music. Those links take visitors to external services governed by their own policies.

Google and YouTube services are governed by their own policies, including Google's privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy.

Those providers may process technical data according to their own policies. We choose these services to operate the public site, not to require visitor accounts on Fairy Tales by Kamu.

Google OAuth and Google API Data

The public story catalog does not require Google sign-in. If Fairy Tales by Kamu offers Google sign-in or Google or YouTube account connection features, we use Google OAuth only for the account holder who chooses to connect and only for permissions shown on the Google consent screen.

Depending on the connected feature and scopes approved by the user, Google user data may include basic profile or account identifiers and Google or YouTube channel, playlist, video, thumbnail, and metadata fields needed to provide the requested story video, publishing, import, verification, or account feature. We do not request contacts, phone, SMS, call logs, microphone, camera, precise location, health, or payment-card data from Google APIs.

We use Google user data only to provide and improve prominent user-facing connected-account features, troubleshoot and secure those features, prevent abuse, and comply with law. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness.

We store Google user data only as long as needed for the authorized feature, security, legal, abuse-prevention, and recordkeeping purposes. Any stored Google authorization tokens are treated as sensitive credentials.

We do not transfer Google user data except to service providers needed to operate the requested feature, for security or legal reasons, as part of a business transaction with required consent where applicable, or at the user's explicit direction. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google's API user-data policy, including the Limited Use requirements: Google API Services User Data Policy.

You can revoke access from the Google connected apps and services page: Google connected apps and services page. You may also request deletion of stored Google-authorized data by emailing support@mprlab.com.

Children and Families

Fairy Tales by Kamu is made for family use. Children should use feedback, email, or external links only with a parent or guardian.

We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information. Children can use the public catalog without connecting a Google account, and any Google or YouTube account connection should be used only by the account holder or an authorized adult. Parents or guardians can contact us to request deletion of information a child may have submitted.

Retention and Requests

We keep operational, analytics, and feedback information only as long as useful for the purposes above or as needed for legal, security, abuse-prevention, and recordkeeping reasons.

To request access, correction, or deletion, email support@mprlab.com. For legal requests, email legal@mprlab.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site, providers, or legal requirements change. The updated page will show the current effective and last-updated dates.
